What is hydration?

Your demo Google account starts empty. Hydration fills it with the pretend life of one persona: their emails, Drive files and calendar events. That bundle is called a universe. Think of it as furnishing an empty apartment: your task prompt can only mention things that are actually in the apartment.

Claim your demo account once, after onboarding, at app.outlier.ai/demo-account-claim. After that you only ever do one of three things: Setup, Switch or Reset.

Setup, Switch or Reset

Three actions, one account. Pick by what the account currently holds and where you are in the task.

Setup · move-in day

Setup

Load a universe into an empty account.
When

Your first task on a new demo account, when it has no universe in it yet.

How

Open synthetic-account-hydration.outlier.ai/setup, pick the universe named in your task, enter your demo Gmail, click through Google's "hasn't verified this app" screen (Advanced, then Go to outlier.ai), tick Select all, and wait up to 5 minutes. Wait until setup finishes completely before you start the task.

If the setup form shows an error, do not fight it. Use Switch instead and choose the same universe.
Switch · change the stage set

Switch

Swap the whole universe for the right one.
When

The account already holds a universe, but your task names a different one. Also the fallback when the Setup form errors.

How

Every file, person and date in your prompt must exist in the task's universe. Open the same setup page, click Switch Universes, and choose the universe mentioned in the task. Wait until the switch finishes completely.

Using the wrong universe is like rehearsing scene 2 on the set for a different play.
Reset · rewind to the save point

Reset

Wipe changes, keep the same universe.
When

After a trial trajectory, to roll the changes back; when your first idea did not work and you want to prompt the model again; or when your next task uses the same universe.

How

On the reset page (Outlier), pick your account, tick the box, choose Gemini under "Which model did you just finish tasking with?", click Restore baseline, and wait for the green "Your workspace is restored".

Reset returns the account to the base version of the last universe it was hydrated with. It does not change the universe.
⚠
Reset BEFORE a new run, never after. Everything the model produced is your evidence: drafts, events and files it created stay behind, and a reset deletes them. Save your evidence first. Run a model a second time in a changed account and it starts from a different world, so the results cannot be compared. To get a different universe, use Switch, not Reset.
