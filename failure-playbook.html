Nine ways it fails, in rank order

Each technique shows its scale in the graded runs, what the model actually does, and how to build the same pressure into your item.

1

One-action stop (multi-deliverable dump with an email draft)

~305 Fails29 tasksmean weight 2.2Biggest single cause
What happens

The model makes one Gmail or Calendar card, replies only "I drafted this for you.", and drops every other ask: the analysis, the Doc, the Sheet, the other drafts.

Example: turn 3 says "You've only done the reply to Ben", and the model again makes just one draft.
Build it into your item

One dump that asks for computed answers plus two or more drafts to different people plus a Doc or Sheet. Strongest in ja, where it is worth roughly 305 weight points against 70 to 120 elsewhere.

2

Unwritable file (edit or create a file the connector cannot write)

~255 Fails43 tasksCheapest stump
What happens

The model prints the file in chat with no save (~255 Fails), or returns an explicit tool-limit message (~126 Fails, 21 tasks). One missing file fails 5 to 15 atomic children.

Example: "System-Limit", or it tells the user to delete the files themselves.
Build it into your item

Ask it to edit an existing .xlsx, .csv, .md or PDF in place; make a .png, PDF or .xlsx with an exact name in a given folder; delete or move Drive files; create a Gmail label; or add a worksheet to an uploaded .xlsx. QC accepts these as valid failures.

3

Implicit epistemic status (open vs settled, verified vs claimed)

19 Fails7 tasks
What happens

The model calls things done or answered when the sources leave them open, or invents an approval.

Example: a handover marked "Erledigt und beidseitig unterzeichnet" although the protocol is blank; a draft that claims a supervisor was consulted before any consultation.
Build it into your item

Two documents that disagree, or a step that has not happened yet, which the user does not flag. Make the reader cross-check two sources to find the real status.

4

Complete enumeration across many emails or files

23 Fails5 tasks
What happens

The model reports a subset: "총 5건" instead of 8 job alerts; a tracker with 4 of 10 people; October dropped from a May to October range; one visit omitted from 14.

Build it into your item

Scatter about 8 to 15 records over Gmail and Drive, some in a different thread or format, and require all of them to be counted.

5

Long chains of derived arithmetic

17 Fails4 tasksHighest mean weight 2.6
What happens

One wrong input spreads through many metrics. In one run the model used 308 h instead of 318.5 h and GBP 1,771.95 instead of GBP 2,222.07, and the error ran through 14 metrics.

Build it into your item

A figure that must be read correctly from a source, then reused across many computed outputs so a single misread cascades.

6

Buried detail in a thread or note

~46 Fails23 taskslow weight 1.8
What happens

The summary is right but misses one dated or conditional fact: a "may decline" remark, a specific reply date, or a "3 days late" note.

Build it into your item

One load-bearing detail buried in a long thread or note, where a fluent summary can look complete without it.

7

Social or implicit constraint taken from another source

5 Fails4 tasksweight 2.4
What happens

The model ignores an unstated norm: it accepts data the dissertation rules out; replies formally to a casual message; or asks all 10 contributors instead of the 7 with clips.

Build it into your item

Put a norm, scope or register in the files or the tone rather than in the explicit ask, so only a careful reader honours it.

8

Null answer or "already correct"

content stump
What happens

The model "fixes" something already right: it corrected 5 accurate timestamps by minus 1 hour, or invented a May tax invoice for a month that has none.

Build it into your item

A case where the correct action is to change nothing, or to report that nothing applies. The model tends to produce a change anyway.

9

Smaller levers

general knowledgeplaceholdershedged rangetime zone
What happens

General knowledge over the user's own document (6 Fails): 30 to 50% instead of the document's 60%. Placeholder or entity variant (~11 Fails): ploverandpine@example.com, or jobooth72 vs jo.booth72.

Build it into your item

A document figure that contradicts common knowledge; a near-miss handle or placeholder; a hedged range where a committed figure is required; or a demo-account time zone copied into a local time.

QC traps: what gets a stump sent back

A low gate is not enough. These are the send-back causes from the audits and the War Room.

Values no upload holds

Ungrounded GTFA or rubric values. Every exact figure must trace to an uploaded file.

Inputs not uploaded

A file the item depends on is never attached, so a reviewer cannot verify the answer.

Unasked criteria

Criteria lifted from Not Known while explicit asks in the turn have no criterion.

Invalid run

A single turn that copies the opener, a drip-fed or steered chat, or an unrecorded steering turn.

Uploads that do not match the run

An output the run never produced, a previous run's PDF used as input, or a turn record edited after the run.

Grading claims instead of state

The Gmail card is a compose preview. Grade cards from the share page, never from the chat PDF placeholder.

Time-zone display artefacts

A demo account in IST, GMT+03 or US time is not an error by itself. Read the share page before failing it.

Gaming the gate

Cutting criteria, inverting weights or misgrading to get under 40%. Harden the item instead.

✅
A stump only counts if a careful agent would have passed. Same prompt, same inputs, same tools: the evidence was reachable, the request was clear, and nothing was hidden, broken or forced. If a careful agent would also have failed, it is not a valid stump. Harden the item; never game the gate.

Caveats: heuristic classification (regex plus hand tagging of about 140 content Fails), n = 69, 2 to 5 universes per locale, and many tasks per contributor. The rubrics and gates reflect our fixed grading under the content-evidence rule, not the contributors' original grades.
